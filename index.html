Our Story

A Table Rooted in Peshawar,
Set for the Modern Guest

Rooted in the rich culinary heritage of Peshawar and inspired by contemporary dining, ZAIQA brings together authentic flavors, refined presentation and warm hospitality. Every dish honours the spice routes and cooking traditions of the frontier, plated with a modern hand.

From slow-smoked karahis to delicately spiced kebabs, our kitchen is a bridge between generations — built on family recipes and elevated by technique.

Meet Our Chef

Chef Imran Khattak

Executive Chef, ZAIQA Signature Dining

Trained in Peshawar's oldest karahi kitchens before honing his craft in Dubai's fine-dining scene, Chef Imran returned home with one mission — to give Peshawari cuisine the presentation it deserves without losing its soul.

"Good food doesn't need to shout. It needs patience, real fire, and respect for where the recipe came from."
